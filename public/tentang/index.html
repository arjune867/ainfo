About AINFO

AINFO is an independent digital publication focused on practical ideas for creating healthier, more comfortable and more sustainable homes and gardens.

What we publish

Our articles cover home gardening, indoor plants, outdoor living, simple DIY projects, home care and sustainable everyday habits. We aim to make useful information clear, approachable and actionable for readers at different experience levels.

Our editorial approach

We prioritize usefulness, clarity and accuracy. Content is reviewed for readability and updated when important information changes. Where advice depends on climate, materials, safety or local conditions, readers should consider their circumstances and consult qualified professionals when appropriate.

Our mission

We believe small, thoughtful improvements can make everyday spaces more welcoming and resilient. AINFO exists to help readers make informed decisions, learn practical skills and enjoy the process.
