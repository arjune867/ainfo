AINFO Home & Garden

Editorial Policy

AINFO aims to publish useful, understandable and responsible information about homes, gardens, plants, DIY and sustainable living.

Research and accuracy

Writers should use credible sources and distinguish established facts from personal experience, estimates or recommendations. Instructions involving tools, chemicals, electricity, structural work or other hazards must be approached cautiously and should direct readers to qualified professionals where appropriate.

Originality

Articles should provide original explanations, organization and practical value. We do not knowingly publish copied work or misleading claims. Images and other media should be original, properly licensed or otherwise used with permission.

Review and updates

Content may be reviewed for accuracy, clarity, usefulness and relevance. When material information changes or a meaningful error is identified, we may update the article.

Corrections and feedback

Readers can report potential errors or suggest improvements through our Contact page. Please include the article URL and supporting information.

Advertising and independence

Advertising may appear separately from editorial content. Commercial relationships should not determine factual conclusions or editorial recommendations. Sponsored material, when published, should be clearly identified.
